Our paper, ["RestoreKV: Recovering Full-Cache Behavior Under Aggressive Query-Agnostic KV Cache Eviction"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01247), is now available on arXiv as a preprint. RestoreKV augments aggressive query-agnostic KV cache eviction with a compact learned restore cache, recovering full-cache behavior under the same budget. It is currently ranked 1st on the [NVIDIA KVPress Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/nvidia/kvpress-leaderboard), which compares KV-cache compression methods for long-context LLM inference.
